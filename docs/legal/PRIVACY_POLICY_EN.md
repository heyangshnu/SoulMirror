# SoulMirror Privacy Policy

**Version: 1.0**  
**Updated: May 28, 2026**

SoulMirror is operated by an **individual developer** (not a company). This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

**Contact:** [your email]

## What we collect

- **Account:** phone number, password (encrypted)
- **Optional profile:** nickname, age range, occupation, concerns
- **Chart & tests:** birth data, test answers, photos (palm), chat messages
- **Social matching (if enabled):** nickname and match tags only—not your phone or full chat history

## How we use data

- Provide readings, reports, AI chat, and optional matching
- Improve the product using de-identified analytics
- **We do not sell** your personal information

## AI & storage

- Data is processed on servers in mainland China
- Necessary content may be sent to third-party AI providers (e.g. DeepSeek) under contractual limits
- Palm photos: originals deleted within about 7 days when that feature is enabled

## Your rights

Access, correct, delete, withdraw consent, and delete your account in the app or by email. We respond within 15 business days.

## Children

Not intended for users under 14 without parental consent.
